## Corrections applied

reference note: [CV-VIDEO](../../../vault/100-mouvement-video/references/102-points-actions-video.md); [flashcards](../../../vault/100-mouvement-video/102-points-actions-video.md). reference note modified before the flashcards.

- **CV-VIDEO-001 –  Axis of annotations**: A point path can be represented by $(x_t,y_t,v_t)$, where $v_t$ describes a visibility according to the protocol. A clip label is a category associated with a window; a time location provides at least one category and an interval $[t_d,t_f]$. Cutting a long video into tagged clips can give rough time cues, but does not automatically create precise boundaries. To compare systems, keep timestamps and match rules with actual events. A point followed on a person is neither a complete identity of the person nor an annotation of action without an additional definition.

- **CV-VIDEO-002 –  Sampling phase**: In a constructed model where an event of duration $d<\Delta$ begins with a uniform phase with respect to a grid of steps $\Delta$, and where an instantaneous capture is sufficient, the probability that at least one sample falls into the event is $d/\Delta$. For 100 ms and a step of 1 s, it is 0,1 under these assumptions. This is not a predicted performance of a real model: exposure, visibility, and persistent clues change the problem. The calculation shows, however, why varying the phase is essential and why a demonstration where the event falls exactly on a frame does not validate a guaranteed reminder.

- **CV-VIDEO-003 –  Minimum time frame for context**: if an estimate for $t$ uses a frame at $t+h$, its availability cannot precede $t+h$, even before transfer and calculation. A latency measurement limited to the network pass would therefore omit at least this context delay. With sliding windows, specify which exits are temporary and which are revised afterwards. Evaluating the first decision and the final trajectory separately makes the comparison readable. A method can remain useful in line with an accepted deadline; the word causal, on the other hand, requires that each decision at the time in question uses only the observations already available according to the announced contract.

- **CV-VIDEO-004 –  Swap and reverse order**: An average $z=L^{-1}\sum_t f(I_t)$ aggregation is mathematically invariant to permutation; a good score obtained in this way can come from visual states or context, without encoding their order. Building pairs like take/pose an object, with close states but a different order, tests another property. check also that permutation does not create an unusual artifact that would cause the model to fall for the wrong reason. Comparing inversion, permutation and cadence by changing only one factor helps to support the use of time without assimilating any temporal sensitivity to a complete understanding of the scene.
